The problem

A customer paid. The merchant’s code said “failed”.

01

Customer pays

The bank is still confirming, so the gateway reports PENDING.

02

Code misreads it

A small bug in the merchant’s callback handler treats PENDING as FAILED.

03

Money arrives anyway

The bank confirms, but the order is already final, so the update is ignored.

04

Refunds and tickets

The customer is charged with no order, and the gateway gets the blame.

The gateway did nothing wrong. The bug lives in merchant integration code, written thousands of different ways, and today it’s found by customers and fixed by support tickets.

Safety

Safe enough for a payments company.

AI never decides a payment

Order states change only through signed gateway callbacks. No money moves.

Independent verifier

A locked test suite the AI can’t read or edit, hash-checked before every run.

Named human approval

Nothing goes live without a reviewer’s name and token, kept in the audit trail.

Knows what isn’t a bug

Forged or mis-signed callbacks are flagged as external incidents, never “fixed”.

Runs on its own

Status checks, Soundbox restarts, customer updates. Logged and verified before anything closes.

Needs a person’s approval

Reconciliation, refund requests, QR re-link, code patches. Approval is tied to the exact action.

Led by a person

Fraud, account freezes, regulatory filings. FIXCHAIN only gathers evidence and routes.

Paytm as first customer

A 30-day pilot that earns its autonomy.

Paytm runs the Soundbox devices, the QR network and the support desk: every part of the loop FIXCHAIN closes. We start with one merchant cohort, Soundbox and QR tickets, read-only.

Week 1

Connect

Sandbox data and read-only access to ticket and device feeds. Agree the success metrics.

Week 2

Shadow

FIXCHAIN triages and groups live tickets in parallel. Agents work as usual; we compare.

Week 3

Assist

Agents get drafts in the merchant’s language. Safe device checks run for a small cohort.

Week 4

Decide

Results against the metrics. Go or no-go on wider rollout and approval-gated actions.

Success criteria: grouping accuracy, time saved per ticket, verified fixes without an agent, and zero unauthorized actions. No money moves, and nothing reaches customers without Paytm’s approval.
